Shift Life Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

The short version: what you put in Shift Life stays on your phone. We have no accounts and no servers for your data, and we never see, touch or view it, for any reason. After the free trial, the app shows ads from Google, and Google's ad service collects its own information from your phone. That's explained below.

Who we are

Shift Life is a phone app for rotating shift work, made by Joe Uhlman (TrenForge). "We" in this policy means the maker of the app. Our contact details are at the end.

What you put in the app

Depending on which parts of the app you use, you may enter:

  • Shifts, rotations, jobs and notes
  • Sleep, including planned and logged times
  • Food, water and caffeine logs, habits and workouts
  • Pay rates, pay estimates and bills
  • Background photos you choose

All of this is saved only on your phone, in the app's own storage. The app doesn't send it to us or to anyone else, and we don't sell it or use it to pick ads.

What we collect

We don't collect anything from inside the app. There's no sign-up, and we have no server that receives your data. That also means we can't recover your data if you lose it, so keep a backup.

Ads from Google

The first 14 days have no ads. After that, unless you've paid to remove them, the app shows ads through Google AdMob, which is run by Google. Google's ad service never gets your shifts, sleep, pay or logs from us. It does collect its own information from your phone, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. According to Google, that includes:

  • Your IP address, which can show your rough location
  • Your Android ad ID, app set ID and, if applicable, other identifiers related to accounts signed in on your phone
  • How you use the app, such as opening it, tapping and watching videos
  • How the app performs, such as launch time, freezes and energy use

We ask Google for ads that aren't personalized. In some places you may also see a message asking for your choice about ads.

During the free trial, and after you remove ads, the app doesn't ask Google for ads. The ad software is still part of the app, though, so we can't promise it never contacts Google at those times.

You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and what its ad software collects. You can reset or delete your ad ID in your Android settings.

Paying to remove ads

Google Play handles any payment. We never get your card details. Google gives app makers order records, such as the order number, date, price and region.

Information from Google Play

Google Play gives app makers statistics such as install counts, ratings and reviews, along with crash and performance reports from phones that allow it. We use these to improve the app. We don't add your app data to them.

When a copy leaves your phone

Your data only leaves your phone in these ways:

  • Backups you send or save. The Email backup and Save backup buttons make a file with your data in it, including pay and sleep information. It goes wherever you choose on your phone's share screen. It isn't encrypted, so only send it to yourself and keep it somewhere safe.
  • Shifts you add to your calendar. If you use Add shifts to my calendar, the app adds your shifts, and your workouts if you use them, to the calendar on your phone that you pick. If that calendar belongs to your Google account, your phone syncs them to Google Calendar. They then follow that calendar's own privacy settings.
  • Calendar files you send. This file holds your shifts, and your workouts if you use them. It goes to the app you pick.
  • Google backup. If Google backup is on for your phone, Android may keep a copy of the app's data in your own Google account. That's between you and Google, and you control it in your phone's settings.

Whichever app or service you send a copy to has its own privacy policy.

What can show on your screen

Reminders can appear on your lock screen, and the home screen widget shows your next shift, next sleep and the week ahead. Anyone who can see your phone could see these. Neither shows pay. You can turn reminders off in the app and remove the widget at any time.

Phone permissions

  • Notifications and exact alarms: to deliver your reminders on time.
  • Internet and network status: for Google's ads. The app's own features don't use the internet.
  • Ad ID: used by Google's ad service, as described above.
  • Starting after a restart: to keep your reminders set after your phone restarts.
  • Calendar: asked for only when you tap Add shifts to my calendar. The app reads the list of your calendars so you can pick one, and reads only its own events in the dates you're adding, so it can update them. It never changes or removes events it didn't add, and nothing from your calendar leaves your phone through the app.

Photos and restore files are opened only when you pick them. The app has no access to your location through GPS, your contacts, your camera or your microphone. You can turn calendar access off in your Android settings at any time. Shifts already added stay in your calendar until you delete them there.

How your data is protected

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which Android keeps apart from other apps. We don't add extra encryption, so your phone's screen lock matters. Google says the data its ad software collects is encrypted on its way to Google.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. We hold no copy, so there's nothing for us to delete and nothing you need to ask us for.
  • Erase everything, in More, clears the data in use. The app's copies of the last 14 days and your background photos stay until you uninstall.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your Android settings, removes all of it from your phone.
  • Files you sent or saved stay wherever you put them, and a copy in Google backup is managed in your Google account.
  • Google decides how long it keeps what its ad service collects. See the links in the ads section.

Children

Shift Life is made for working adults and isn't meant for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect anything from children, and the app itself collects nothing from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here with a new date. If a change affects how your data is handled, we'll also say so in the app.
